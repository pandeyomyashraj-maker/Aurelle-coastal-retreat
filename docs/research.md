# Research notes — AURELLE concept

Checked 14 October 2026. These are inspiration patterns, not copied layouts or content.

## Reference patterns observed

- **Atlantis The Royal:** the official stay pages use distinct stay categories, filters, room details, amenities/inclusions, and direct exploration calls to action. The official World Travel Tech Awards page lists Atlantis The Royal as the 2025 World's Best Hotel Website. References: https://www.atlantis.com/dubai/atlantis-the-royal/stay and https://worldtraveltechawards.com/award/world-best-hotel-website/2025
- **Anantara:** the World Travel Tech Awards lists Anantara Hotels, Resorts and Spas as the 2025 World's Best Luxury Hotel Brand Website. Reference: https://worldtraveltechawards.com/award/world-best-luxury-hotel-brand-website/2025
- **Six Senses Vana:** its official site frames stays around personalised wellness, consultation, programs and guest experience rather than only room imagery. Reference: https://www.sixsenses.com/en/hotels-resorts/asia-the-pacific/india/vana

## What this project applies

- A split-screen scroll-driven visual journal, suite selector, and hospitality narrative that connects architecture, wellness, dining, and landscape.
- A new **Your Rhythm / Stay Composer** interaction lets visitors choose stillness, discovery, or restoration and see an illustrative three-moment itinerary; the selection can prefill the local demo enquiry form. This borrows the general idea of personalised stay planning, not any proprietary UI.
- A concept guestbook uses original sample copy from fictional personas, with a prominent disclosure. It contains no fake stars, review-site logos, or public-figure endorsements.
- Performance decisions favour real CSS/JS effects, scroll-linked updates, and image lazy loading over a heavy WebGL scene.

## Limitations

AURELLE is fictional. Room sizes, services, the map coordinates, the itinerary, and hotel availability are illustrative. The reservation form is local-only and does not send or store submissions. Images load from remote photo CDNs; inspect and document each source/licence before public commercial launch.
